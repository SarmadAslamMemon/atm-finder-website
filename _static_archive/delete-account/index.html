Account & Data Deletion

App Package: dev.varbox.atmfinder  |  Compliance: Google Play User Data & Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play Developer policies, users of ATM Finder Pakistan who registered an optional account for cloud bookmark synchronization can request the permanent deletion of their account and associated data.

What Happens When You Request Deletion?

Data Permanently Deleted:

  • Account Profile: Your registered name, email address, password hash, and authentication tokens.
  • Cloud Bookmarks: All saved ATM locations, favorites, and custom search radius preferences stored in your cloud profile.
  • User Associations: Any internal links connecting your user identity to historical community reports.

Data Retained:

  • Anonymous Community Reports: Historical status reports (e.g., "Cash available at Mall Road") are retained purely in an aggregated, anonymous format without any personal identifier, to preserve historical directory accuracy for local users.

How to Request Deletion

You can delete your account using either of the following two methods:

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Instant)

  1. Open the ATM Finder Pakistan app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation.
  3. Tap Account Settings → Delete Account.
  4. Confirm your request. Your cloud account and bookmarks will be wiped immediately.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request Form

If you have uninstalled the app or prefer to request deletion via the web, submit the form below or email privacy@varbox.dev with the subject "Account Deletion Request".

Please confirm that you understand this action cannot be undone. All bookmarked ATMs will be permanently removed.

Processing Timeline: In-app deletion is instantaneous. Web and email requests are verified and permanently purged from our production databases within 7 business days. A confirmation email will be sent once the process is complete.
